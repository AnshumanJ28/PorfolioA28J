01 — Overview

About

I am an AI & Machine Learning engineer who likes to build systems which survive beyond the boundaries of notebooks. My definition of a good model does not include only achieving high performance but also making it reproducible, versioned, monitorable, testable, and working under different data.

Almost all of my projects are related to production ML where I design ML pipelines, deploy full machine learning solutions, observe model drifts, and build systems which are maintainable over time. I love doing engineering as much as I train models.

In addition to production-related projects, I build some projects for fun and challenge myself. AlphaZ0 is such an example; it is a self-play chess engine project and a deep study of reinforcement learning motivated by a game I love to play.

When I am not busy with AI projects, I solve problems and try to design systems or play chess.
